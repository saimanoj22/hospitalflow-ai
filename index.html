03

Train on days 1–77, evaluate only on days 78–98. Compare holdout MAE against a constant mean. The benchmark is synthetic only.

TRANSPARENCY & DESIGN

Built to simulate. Not to overclaim.

A working, reproducible digital twin prototype with explicit assumptions, testable behavior and limitations.

OPEN SOURCE · MIT

Virtual operational state

Individual synthetic patients move through time-stepped ED queues, triage, care, observation, ICU and discharge. Each step updates resource occupancy and time-to-care measures.

Dynamic synchronization

A deterministic pseudo-random synthetic event stream enters the digital twin on every simulated minute. Inputs can change while the model continues running.

Counterfactual experiments

Bounded scenarios compare different allocations of the same float-staff pool. Exogenous arrivals use an independent random stream for consistency between interventions.

AI/ML component

The forecast studio loads an actual trained harmonic ridge-regression model, including held-out error estimates. Staffing uses interpretable exhaustive optimization, not a language model.

SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY

Assumptions and boundaries

All arrivals, acuity categories, service times, dispositions and hospital capacities are demonstrative synthetic assumptions.
The model is a minute-resolution stochastic queueing simulation; no claims of physical fidelity or validated clinical decision support.
Patient-level identities exist only in browser memory; no medical records or external services are accessed.
Optimizer outcomes depend on the simulation rules, selected scenario and seed. They do not establish improvements at real hospitals.
Production use would require hospital event-log calibration, prospective validation, clinician review, auditability and governance.
